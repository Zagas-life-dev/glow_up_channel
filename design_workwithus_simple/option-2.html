Option 2: just show us.

Most people already have the thing: a flyer on their phone, a link, or they can just say it. So we don't ask any questions up front. They show it to us, the AI fills in the form, and they only answer "Is this right?" and give one phone number.

Nobody needs to know what a "listing", "opportunity" or "revenue share" is. The AI works out whether it's a job, an event, an opportunity or a course, and our team checks every one before it goes live anyway.

How it works

1 · Show usFour giant buttons: 📷 Photo, 🔗 Link, 🎤 Talk, ✍️ Type. Or skip the site completely and send it on WhatsApp.
2 · We read itThe AI pulls out the name, organiser, date, place, link and type. The person watches it fill in line by line, so they know something is happening.
3 · "Is this right?"They see it exactly as it will look on UP. One big "Yes". Any line can be tapped and fixed. Anything the AI couldn't find shows in red and gets asked as its own question.
4 · Only the missing bitsAt most one or two big yes/no questions, e.g. "Do people pay to come?" or "7 days or 30 days?" (only if it costs money).
5 · Your WhatsApp numberThe only box they have to type in. Receipt and "it's live" updates go there.
6 · Send / PayOne button showing the price. Paystack if it's paid. Done screen says "We'll WhatsApp you".

What it needs to build

An AI "read this" endpointThe backend already calls Gemini (free tier) for tagging (lib/admin/tagging.ts). Reading a flyer photo, a link or a transcript into our fields is the same model with a different prompt, returning the existing DETAIL_FIELDS shape.
VoiceRecord in the browser, send the audio to the same Gemini call (it takes audio). No separate speech service.
WhatsApp instead of emailToday the receipt goes out by email (SES). Sending it on WhatsApp needs the WhatsApp Business API, or for v1: keep email optional and have the team message by hand from the review queue.
UnchangedPrices, Paystack, the order book, the admin review queue. The last screen still sends the same SubmissionPayload.
