The layer on top of the pages, in the same language.

The pages now speak UP Design v1. The things that open over them still don't: modals, bottom sheets, permission prompts, the gift and extreme pop-ups, toasts, banners, menus, empty and error states. This proposal redraws every one of them that a user can still reach. The content, the triggers and the flows stay the same; only how they look and where they sit changes.

What's covered

Dialog on desktop, sheet on phoneSame content, two containers. Desktop centres a 24px-radius card; phones slide a sheet up from the bottom with a grab handle, in reach of the thumb.
One orange actionEvery overlay has at most one solid orange button. Dismiss is a plain text button or the ✕, never a second loud button.
Red only destroysThe only red in the product is the delete confirmation. Errors elsewhere are calm text, not alarms.
Navy means "this is special"Only the extreme announcement, the gift and the sign-up nudge get a navy hero with the card-stack tiles. Everything else is a white card.

The overlay system

  • Scrim: navy at 55% (dark: near-black at 72%), no blur. The blur in glass-surface and backdrop-blur-sm goes: it costs frames on low-end Android and muddies the page behind.
  • Dialog: white card, 24px radius, 22/24px padding. Header = optional icon tile + Unbounded 20px title + one-line reason. Footer is separated by a hairline and right-aligned: plain dismiss, then the one orange action.
  • Sheet: 28px top radius, grab handle, same header/footer order, buttons full width and stacked with the orange one on top.
  • Closing: ✕, Esc, tapping the scrim and swiping the sheet down all dismiss. Two exceptions need an explicit choice: the delete confirmation, and any form with unsaved typing.
  • Focus: focus moves into the overlay and returns to the control that opened it; the orange focus ring from inputs is used on every control inside.
  • Motion: dialogs fade + rise 8px in 180ms; sheets slide in 240ms. Reduced motion: fade only.

One interruption at a time Proposed

Today each prompt runs its own timer (install, push after install, two location prompts, gift, extreme announcement, guest sign-up). They can stack on the same visit. Proposed: a single queue that shows at most one unasked-for pop-up per visit, in this order. Anything the person triggered themselves (return sheet, add to playlist, confirmations) is never queued.

Not redesigned on purpose

These still use the old styles but nothing in the app renders them, or they belong to deprecated features. They should be deleted rather than restyled:

  • content-share-composer, connection-requests-modal, connections-list-modal, post-card, post-composer, reply-card — community posts and channels are deprecated.
  • promotion/PromotionModal, PromotionButton, payment-details, wallet — promotions are never billed.
  • navbar, footer, hero-carousel, featured-card, enhanced-search, newsletter-popup, playlist-invitations, locked-in-indicator, provider-nav, recommendations/UnifiedRecommendations and the location-permission-card it alone uses — no importer anywhere.
