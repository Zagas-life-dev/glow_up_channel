# AESDI A4 2026: promo storyboard v1

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical, ~63s, HeyGen voiceover, ducked music bed, SFX on reveals. Content sits in the top ~80% so burned-in captions (if accepted) have the bottom rail.

**Spine:** *the lit continent.* In frame 1 an outline of Africa fills with points of gold light (talent everywhere), and most of them go dark on "Opportunity isn't." In the sign-off every point is lit. Gold ribbon sweeps (from the poster's swoosh) carry the transitions, always moving upward.

**Brand (from the poster):** deep green `#0B3A24` field, darker `#062417`, gold `#D4A437`, light gold `#F1D27A`, cream `#FBF3DC` text. Display is Fraunces 900 (a warm serif, close to the logo's wordmark). Labels and data are Oswald (condensed caps, close to the poster's "NOMINATIONS…" line). Small body text is Montserrat.

**Bans:** no full-bleed stretched photos (the sources are small, so they appear as framed prints); no neon or cyan; no fake UI; no invented people, quotes or numbers; no static end card. Motion failures to avoid are the *slideshow* (every beat a fresh card with nothing persisting) and the *screensaver* (drift that says nothing).

**Held frame:** frame 9 on "to open more doors". The CTA block settles and nothing moves for about 1s.

**Truthfulness:** every photo is AESDI's own, from the 2024 and 2025 editions and the July 2026 AI training, taken from the event brief. Every figure is from the brief (§5, §8). The AI-training photos are never labelled as Street to School.
