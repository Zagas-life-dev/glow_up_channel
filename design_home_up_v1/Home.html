UP · Signed-in home · Design proposal v1

Same feed, same flow — now it looks like UP.

The landing page speaks navy, orange and Unbounded. The moment someone signs in, the home feed switches to a generic grey card list in sky, emerald and violet. This proposal brings the signed-in home into UP Design v1 without moving a single step of how the feed works.

24 Sep 2026 Built on UP Design v1 tokens Source: app/home-client.tsx · components/feed-card.tsx v1.1 — decisions settled, dark mode added Proposal only — no code changed
At a glance

What moves, and what doesn't

The flow stays

  • Fixed tab bar: For You, Opportunities, Jobs, Events, Resources, and the country picker
  • A one-line greeting, shown only on For You
  • The extreme campaign always first, a sponsored item after every four posts, "Sponsored" always shown above the card
  • Clicking the title opens the listing. Like, save, add to playlist and share work as they do now
  • Infinite scroll, skeletons while loading, and the end-of-feed line

The look changes

  • Four off-brand type colours become UP-palette chips told apart by lightness
  • Red/amber deadline rails become orange pills that escalate by urgency
  • The navy sidebar becomes the "place" you're in, and the active tab becomes a navy pill
  • The extreme campaign becomes the page's one elevated navy card
  • Unbounded is used only for the greeting, the match number and the lead title

Decided

  • Lime stays as the Events colour
  • The extreme campaign keeps its own navy look, because it's meant to grab attention
  • The desktop sidebar goes navy
  • Dark mode is now designed (see below)
  • All four apply to every user-facing page, not just home

Dark mode · new

The same home, after dark

The page background drops below brand navy (#070A1C), so the navy sidebar and the extreme card still read as distinct areas instead of blending into the page. Cards lift to a lighter navy. Orange and lime keep their full strength, only their tints get quieter. Solid-navy chips (jobs, active tab, active nav) flip to cream so they don't disappear.

Decided · 24 Sep 2026

Four calls, settled

1

Lime is the Events colour

Used as a tint behind the events chip and never as text colour, matching the landing page. Kept.

2

The extreme campaign gets its own look

The navy card with the orange and lime tiles and one orange action. It's meant to catch the eye. Every other promoted tier still looks like an ordinary card, and the "Sponsored · Featured" label stays in the same spot. Kept.

3

Navy sidebar on desktop

The same navy sidebar appears on every signed-in page. Mobile keeps the light bottom nav. Approved.

4

Dark mode

Designed above as a proposed dark theme for UP Design v1. Added for review.

These apply across the platform, not just home

They cover every user-facing page. Provider and admin dashboards are out of scope for now.

  • Lime = Events everywhere a content type is shown: feed, hub lists, search results, playlists, tracker, detail pages.
  • The extreme look applies on every surface where the campaign appears first: For You, the Opportunities / Jobs / Events / Resources tabs, and the hub pages.
  • Navy sidebar on every signed-in desktop page.
  • Dark mode covers every user-facing page, using the tokens in the table above.
